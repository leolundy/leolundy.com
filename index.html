The research

From endurance
to evidence.

The running is where the question began: what can sustained endurance participation tell us about successful ageing?
The Multi-Marathon Study at Trinity College Dublin examines participation, psychology, mental health, cognition and physiology in a distinctive international endurance population, then brings those strands together around ageing well.
